ABOUT

Welcome to the Alumni Management Portal for Batch 2020-2021. This platform is designed to help the school administration efficiently manage alumni members, track events, and maintain communication with our graduates.

Through this portal, alumni can stay updated on school news, upcoming events, and networking opportunities. Administrators can manage event schedules, record attendance, and access alumni data securely.

Our goal is to foster a strong alumni community that remains connected with Carigara National Vocational School. By providing this platform, we aim to encourage collaboration, mentorship, and continued engagement among our graduates.

For further inquiries or support, you can contact us directly at batch2021@gmail.com. We are committed to assisting alumni and ensuring their experience with the school remains meaningful and productive.
