CNVS

Carigara National Vocational School (CNVS), formerly Carigara School of Fisheries, is located in Barugohay Norte, Carigara, Philippines. CNVS is a vocational school offering programs in agriculture, food processing, drafting, and garment production for junior high school. Senior high school offers both technical-vocational-livelihood (TVL) and academic strands, focusing on the General Academic Strand as well as agriculture and food processing.

House Rules

1. Time is Gold. Be punctual.
2. Everyone's ideas, suggestions, and decisions are appreciated.
3. Cooperation is a must — we should work as one.
4. Be present in every meeting. Fines apply for unexcused absences: Php300.00 for Elected Officers and Php150.00 for Board of Directors.
5. Representatives are accepted for Board of Directors, but not for Elected Officers.
6. Be responsible in every assigned task.
7. Show respect to the association and its members. Uphold dignity and honor.
